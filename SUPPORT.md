# Getting Support

Thank you for using webgrip projects. Here's how to get help depending on what you need.

---

## 🐛 Found a bug?

Open an **Issue** in the relevant repository. Please include:

- A clear description of the unexpected behaviour
- Steps to reproduce
- What you expected vs. what actually happened
- Relevant logs, configs, or screenshots

If you're unsure which repo owns the problem, open the issue here in `.github` and we'll triage it.

---

## 💡 Have a question?

Use **[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/webgrip/discussions)** — specifically the **Q&A** category. This keeps answers discoverable for others who run into the same thing.

Please search existing discussions before opening a new one.

---

## 🏗️ Infrastructure / homelab questions

For anything related to the [homelab-cluster](https://github.com/webgrip/homelab-cluster) (Flux, HelmRelease, Talos, SOPS, GitOps patterns), open a Q&A discussion there directly. The cluster is GitOps-managed — if something isn't working, the answer is almost always in the manifests.

---

## 🔒 Security vulnerability?

Please **do not** open a public issue. Instead, follow the [Security Policy](./SECURITY.md) and report it privately via GitHub's [Security Advisories](https://github.com/webgrip/.github/security/advisories/new) feature, or email [ryan@webgrip.nl](mailto:ryan@webgrip.nl).

---

## 📬 Direct contact

For anything that doesn't fit the above — partnerships, consulting, or sensitive matters — reach out at **[ryan@webgrip.nl](mailto:ryan@webgrip.nl)** or visit **[webgrip.nl](https://webgrip.nl)**.

---

## ⏱️ Response time expectations

Webgrip is maintained primarily by one person alongside client work. Responses typically happen within a few days. Issues and discussions on active projects are prioritised over archived or experimental ones.

Patience is appreciated — and so are well-formed questions.
